FRONTEND STRUCTURE
==================

/src
  /@types
    - auth.ts (tipos de autenticação)
    - api.ts (tipos de API)
  
  /components (reutilizáveis)
    - Input.tsx
    - Button.tsx
    - Card.tsx
    - Toast.tsx
    - UrlItem.tsx
  
  /context (estado global)
    - AuthContext.tsx
    - ToastContext.tsx (opcional)
  
  /hooks (custom hooks)
    - useAuth.ts
    - useLocalStorage.ts
  
  /pages (telas)
    - Login.tsx
    - Register.tsx
    - Dashboard.tsx
  
  /services (API)
    - api.ts (interceptador com refresh token)
    - auth.ts (login, register, logout)
    - shortUrl.ts (shorten, list, delete)
  
  /styles (CSS global)
    - globals.css
    - variables.css
  
  /utils (funções)
    - validation.ts
    - constants.ts
  
  App.tsx (router)
  main.tsx (entry)
